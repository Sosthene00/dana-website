Dana Wallet

Send bitcoin to the people you love, anytime, anywhere

  • Bitcoin is your money: send and receive as you like, don't ask permission
  • Claim your Dana address, an email-like address that everyone can use to send you money
  • Organize your contacts book, sending money is always 2 taps away

How does it work?

  • Silent Payment is a new protocol for payment codes that makes address reuse impossible and improves bitcoin user experience
  • Powered by privacy preserving Blindbit oracle backend: use a public instance or bootstrap your own
  • Works with BIP353 to make sending and receiving bitcoins as easy as sending an email
